JiT noise recipe: FID, brightness and spread

Base model (8 blocks, width 360), seed 2, 100,000 steps on the 3090. Scores: 10,000 samples, 16 Euler steps. Probe: 96 samples, 20 Euler steps, averaged (EMA) weights. Back to train-viz

  • Baseline: logit-normal noise levels, mean 0, std 1.5.
  • JiT recipe: noise levels of JiT: logit-normal mean 0.586, std 0.8 (more low-noise inputs).
  • JiT + 5% pure noise: JiT recipe, and 5% of the inputs at noise levels 0.9 to 1.
  • Noise-mean spread: baseline noise levels, and each noise sample gets one constant offset: 0.0136 × randn on all pixels.

The JiT recipe trains almost never at noise levels above 0.9 (0.025% of the inputs), so its first prediction is dark and the samples stay dark. 5% of inputs at nearly pure noise fixes the brightness but not the spread between images.
